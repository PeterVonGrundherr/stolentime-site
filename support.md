## Common questions

**My apps aren't locking.**
Check Settings → Screen Time → Stolen Time and confirm permission is granted. The shield only applies once your daily timer has been exceeded.

**The donation went through but my apps didn't unlock.**
Force-close Stolen Time and reopen it. The unlock is triggered the moment Pledge confirms the payment. If it still doesn't unlock, email us with the donation amount and approximate time so we can check.

**I want to change my daily limit / unlock cost.**
These are locked at setup. Delete the app and reinstall to set new values. Your charity choice and Notifications permission can be changed in the app.

**I turned off Stolen Time and want to turn it back on.**
Delete the app and reinstall. The kill switch is intentionally one-way.

**Where does my donation go?**
Directly to the nonprofit you chose, via Pledge. Stolen Time does not take a cut.

**Can I get a refund on a donation?**
Donations go to the charity, not us. Contact Pledge for refund questions: support@pledge.to.
