# Privacy Policy — Stolen Time

**Last updated: 29 May 2026**

Stolen Time is built to collect as little about you as possible. This page explains what that means in plain language.

## Short version

- We don't run a backend. There is no Stolen Time account, no profile, no email collection.
- We don't track you. There is no analytics, no advertising SDK, no crash reporter.
- Your choice of apps to lock stays on your device. It's stored in iOS's Family Controls system, which we cannot access.
- Donations are processed by Pledge (pledge.to). Your card details are entered into Pledge's checkout form. We never see them.
- We do not sell, share, or transmit your personal information to any third party.

## What stays on your device

All of the following are stored locally on your iPhone or iPad and never sent anywhere:

- The apps you chose to track.
- The daily minute budget you set.
- The amount you donate per unlock.
- The charity you selected.
- The total amount you have donated through the app (a running counter shown in the Profile screen).
- Family Controls tokens generated by Apple to represent your selected apps. These are opaque identifiers that even we cannot turn back into app names — only iOS can.

These values live in iOS's app storage and an App Group container. They are deleted automatically if you delete the app.

## Permissions we ask for

### Screen Time / Family Controls — required

We use Apple's Family Controls framework to lock the apps you choose when their daily timer is exceeded. iOS handles all of this on-device. Apple's documentation explains what Family Controls can and cannot see: it gives apps the ability to *select* apps and *enforce* shields, but does not give apps access to usage data, browsing history, or the contents of any app.

We use these specific Family Controls APIs:

- `FamilyActivityPicker` so you can choose which apps to track.
- `DeviceActivity` to know when your daily budget has been reached.
- `ManagedSettings` to apply and remove the lock shield.

We do not request the `child` authorization variant. We do not run in a parental-control context.

### Notifications — optional

If you allow them, we send a single notification when your apps lock, so you can find your way back to Stolen Time and decide whether to donate. You can deny notifications during onboarding or revoke them later from iOS Settings → Notifications → Stolen Time. The lock still works either way.

## What we do NOT collect

- We do not collect your name, email, phone number, or any account credentials.
- We do not collect device identifiers, IP addresses, or location.
- We do not collect app usage statistics, screen-time reports, or any data about apps other than the ones you explicitly add to the tracked list.
- We do not embed analytics SDKs (no Firebase, no Mixpanel, no Amplitude, no Segment).
- We do not embed advertising SDKs.
- We do not embed crash reporters.

## Donations

When you tap the lock and choose to donate, Stolen Time hands you off to the Pledge donation form rendered in an in-app browser. From that point forward you are interacting with Pledge, not us.

- Pledge handles your card details, Apple Pay token, or PayPal account.
- Pledge processes the payment and routes the donation to the nonprofit you selected.
- Pledge has its own privacy policy at https://www.pledge.to/privacy that governs that interaction.

The only information that returns to Stolen Time after a successful donation is the dollar amount, which we add to the local "Donated" counter shown in your Profile. That counter is stored on your device and not transmitted anywhere.

## Nonprofit search

When you search for or browse charities, Stolen Time sends the search term to Pledge's public organization-search API to fetch matching nonprofits. The search term itself is not personal data, but it is sent over the internet (encrypted via HTTPS) to Pledge's servers.

## Children

Stolen Time is not directed at children under 13. The app uses Family Controls in the `individual` authorization mode, which is intended for an adult managing their own device, not a parent managing a child's device.

## Changes to this policy

If we change anything here, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top and explain what changed. Material changes will be noted in the app's release notes.

## Jurisdiction

Stolen Time is offered worldwide through the Apple App Store. We are headquartered in Germany. Where applicable, you have rights under the GDPR, CCPA, and similar privacy laws — including the right to know what we hold about you, the right to deletion, and the right to object. Since we do not maintain any server-side store of personal data tied to you, the answer to most of these requests will be "we have nothing on you," but we are happy to confirm in writing if you ask.
